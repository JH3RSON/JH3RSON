## 👋 Hey, I'm Ima

I build Android apps and tinker with custom ROMs for Pixel phones. Most of what you'll find here started as something I wanted for myself: apps that respect your privacy, work offline and feel premium.

I make them with a lot of care, and I don't ask for anything in return. If something I built is useful to you, that's already the best part for me. Thank you for being here. 💙

If you feel like supporting my work, donations genuinely help me keep going, but they're never expected. Enjoy the projects, and feel free to open an issue or just say hi.
